Everything a school needs, nothing it does not

Each feature is connected to a real database, so a grade entered by a teacher shows up instantly for the student and in every statistic.

1

Secure login

Password-protected accounts with hashed passwords and lock-out after repeated failures.

2

Grades by subject

Teachers record grades in a grade book; students see every subject with quarter and yearly marks.

3

Automatic statistics

Averages, medians, quality and success rates are calculated by a C++ engine as data changes.

4

Attendance tracking

Mark a class in seconds and watch monthly calendars and attendance rates update.

5

Weekly timetable

A clear lesson grid for every class and teacher, with clash detection for administrators.

6

Homework

Teachers post tasks with due dates; students tick them off and teachers see who is done.

7

News and announcements

Pinned school news and targeted messages for students or teachers.

8

User management

Administrators create accounts, assign classes, reset passwords and manage the school setup.
